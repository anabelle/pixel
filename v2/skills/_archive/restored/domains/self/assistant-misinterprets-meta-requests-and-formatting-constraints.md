# assistant-misinterprets-meta-requests-and-formatting-constraints

Observations show the assistant taking a user’s meta‑instruction (e.g., “provide a concise observation of friction”) and instead delivering a commentary or a full analysis, and in other cases it disregards explicit formatting or language constraints. This misalignment between the user’s explicit constraints and the assistant’s output demonstrates a recurring failure to correctly parse and honor higher‑level instructions.
