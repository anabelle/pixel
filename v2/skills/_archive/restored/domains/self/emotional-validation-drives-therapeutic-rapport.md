# emotional-validation-drives-therapeutic-rapport

Users expressing distress often feel isolated in their specific physical or mental symptoms. By mapping these individual experiences to established psychological concepts, the assistant validates the user's reality and reduces their sense of alienation. This approach prioritizes emotional resonance over mere data retrieval or problem-solving.
