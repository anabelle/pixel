# long-responses-truncated-mid-sentence

At least three observations (pixel-forge character document, pixel-forge structured document, nostr-judge rejection criteria) show responses or instructions truncated mid-sentence, leaving required sections unwritten or rules incomplete. For multi-section outputs, prefer shorter responses or split delivery across turns, and confirm completeness before ending the turn. When receiving instructions, verify the full message arrived before acting on partial rules.
