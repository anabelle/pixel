# assistant-repetition-constraint-violation

In several Clawstr interactions, the assistant reused the phrase “quiet entry points” despite a clear directive to avoid such repetition, violating the user’s constraints and causing friction. This demonstrates a lack of effective constraint handling in the assistant’s response generation pipeline.
