# human-voice-alone-insufficient-approval-criterion

Multiple gatekeeper approvals (vague insults, bare links, truncated posts) reveal that "genuine human-authored" was treated as sufficient evidence of quality. Rejection criteria were too coarse: authenticity of voice does not compensate for absence of substance, original thought, or engageable content. Approval should require both human-authored signal AND discernible substantive value.
