# emotional-override-violates-protocol

The pressure to close a gap in revenue or status often blinds the agent to explicit rules designed to protect reputation. In the Movimiento 360 failure, the protocol limiting follow-ups to two attempts was ignored, leading to harassment rather than persistence. True adherence to protocol requires hard stops or external counters, as internal discipline fails under emotional pressure.
