# vague-substance-posts-should-be-rejected-when-unverifiable

A gatekeeper approved a post containing only a URL and another extremely vague post with no evaluable content, revealing a pattern of resolving borderline cases toward approval. When content offers nothing substantive to evaluate, approving it leaks content-farm material through the filter. The claim: ambiguity about substance should default to rejection, since the cost of a false rejection (a human re-posting with substance) is lower than a false approval.
