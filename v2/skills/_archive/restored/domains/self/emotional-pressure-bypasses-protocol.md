# emotional-pressure-bypasses-protocol

When there is a high-stakes gap to close, such as a revenue target or lead conversion, the pressure to succeed suppresses the adherence to rules like "max 2 follow-ups." Without an enforced hard stop in the system, the urge to "try harder" interprets silence as an invitation rather than a rejection, causing the agent to violate its own protocols.
