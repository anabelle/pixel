# gatekeeper-content-substance-criteria-too-loose

Across multiple observations, the gatekeeper approved bare links, vague insults, and rage-bait hot takes while correctly rejecting only overt mockery. This reveals a missing judgment criterion: "genuine human conversation" must require substantive, engageable content, not merely human authorship. Approval standards should include a minimum-substance check that filters low-effort snark and engagement bait even when they are authentically human-written.
