# assistant-fails-to-adhere-to-content-originality-constraints

Multiple captured observations show that the assistant ignores explicit instructions to avoid repeated phrasing and to keep responses short. Instead, it retains prior wording and supplies lengthy XML blocks or verbose explanations, leading to user frustration over non‑compliance with formatting and brevity requirements. This pattern indicates a systemic difficulty in balancing originality, brevity, and constraint satisfaction.
