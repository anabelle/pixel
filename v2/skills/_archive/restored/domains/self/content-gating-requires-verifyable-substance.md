# content-gating-requires-verifyable-substance

Multiple frictions show the gatekeeper approving vague posts with unverifiable media, rejecting a draft that was never provided, and requesting run URLs/read access when a claim couldn't be independently verified. The pattern: when substance is missing or unobservable, the correct move is to withhold judgment and request the missing artifact rather than guess. Text-only or assumption-based filtering systematically misfires when the claim depends on unseen content.
