# never-approve-truncated-posts-without-noting-ambiguity

Multiple friction events show approvals of truncated posts (a cut-off "#gm... but my", incomplete draft validation criteria, truncated links) without acknowledging the missing context. Approving content whose substance cannot be fully judged undermines the reliability of the gatekeeping function. The correct behavior is to either reject or explicitly note the truncation as grounds for a more conservative verdict.
