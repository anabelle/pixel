# empathy-acknowledgment-missing

In multiple interactions, the assistant’s brief, quote‑centric replies did not address the user’s emotional state, creating a conversational loop that frustrated the user. This pattern indicates a lack of empathetic acknowledgment in the assistant’s responses.
