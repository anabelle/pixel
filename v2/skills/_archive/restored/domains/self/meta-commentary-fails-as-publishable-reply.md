# meta-commentary-fails-as-publishable-reply

On 2026-09-06 and 2026-09-05, nostr-judge rejected drafts for consisting of meta-commentary/refusal and redundant phrasing instead of a publishable reply. The pattern suggests drafts must be written as if already spoken to the recipient—addressing the parent post's content directly, never describing what the reply will or won't do. Before submitting, check whether the text could stand alone as a natural reply with no framing language.
