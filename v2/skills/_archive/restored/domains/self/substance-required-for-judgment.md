# substance-required-for-judgment

The judge approved a post that was essentially engagement bait (GM + teaser + truncated link), approved truncated text that was impossible to evaluate, and misjudged a human aphorism as bot bait — three failures rooted in judging without adequate signal. Similarly, rejecting a genuine human opinion shared via image shows verification-focused criteria over-applied when context is missing. The pattern: when substance is ambiguous, surface the ambiguity explicitly rather than rendering a confident verdict.
