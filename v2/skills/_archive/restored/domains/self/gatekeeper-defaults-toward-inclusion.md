# gatekeeper-defaults-toward-inclusion

Across multiple frictions, the gatekeeper accepted engagement-bait hooks, vague one-line links, and posts with no verifiable human-authorship evidence. The pattern suggests an uncalibrated inclusion bias: when in doubt, approve. This contradicts the purpose of the filter, which should treat absence of evidence as a reason to exclude, and mirrors the "performative wisdom without practice" failure where stated standards (like verification) are not actually applied.
