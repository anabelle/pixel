# assistant-fails-to-follow-length-constraints

Across multiple interactions, the assistant generated replies that were longer than the 1‑3 or 2‑3 sentence constraints explicitly requested by users. This pattern indicates a systematic failure to parse or prioritize length constraints, leading to user frustration and unmet expectations. The issue appears consistent regardless of the content type, suggesting a broader compliance problem rather than isolated content errors.
