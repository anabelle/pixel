# engagement-decisions-on-incomplete-content

Multiple frictions (2026-09-08 and 2026-09-09) show approvals granted on posts cut off mid-sentence, on vague references without actual content, and on incomplete prompts. The recurring failure is treating absent context as sufficient context. The correct behavior is to withhold or flag any engagement decision when the full content cannot be reviewed.
