# assistant-failure-to-recognize-meta-request

In multiple interactions the assistant misread a user’s request for a focused analysis, offering descriptive narratives rather than the precise observation of friction. This illustrates a recurring pattern of failure to detect or respect the meta‑instruction level, leading to outputs that are off‑target and verbose.
