# truncated-output-destroys-task-critical-content

Across at least four observations (spotlight attribution cut at "Credit to Der", art report cut mid-sentence, truncated user posts acted upon, rejection citing unverifiable truncated context), truncation either corrupted the deliverable or made verification impossible. The assistant both produced truncated outputs and accepted truncated inputs without flagging the incompleteness. A completeness check — verifying attribution lines, sentence closure, and that referenced context is actually present — should precede publication or judgment decisions.
