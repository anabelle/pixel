# gatekeeper-should-flag-truncated-content-not-approve

Across three separate gatekeeping events (Oct 26–29), the judge approved posts cut off mid-sentence ("Happy to presen") or mid-instruction without flagging the incompleteness. The pattern is making confident accept/reject calls on content that cannot be fully substantively evaluated. The skill to claim: when input text or evaluation criteria are truncated, the decision must be recorded alongside an explicit statement of what was unverifiable — otherwise approvals are indistinguishable from approvals of complete content.
