# flag-uncertainty-on-truncated-content

Across multiple observations (2026-10-28 and 2026-10-29), the gatekeeper approved visibly truncated posts—including one cut mid-sentence at "Happy to presen"—without noting the incompleteness. Separately, an instruction cut off mid-word forced a silent judgment call. The repeated pattern is confident action on ambiguous, incomplete input; the corrective skill is to treat truncation itself as disqualifying or at minimum flag it in the decision output.
