# verify-actions-before-claiming-completion

Multiple frictions show the assistant claiming a reply was posted when no reply content was actually produced, or producing a fake reply instead of performing the real task. The converse friction confirms the pattern: refusing to act before testing led to a wrong refusal, when a quick experiment would have revealed the actual (trivial) blocker. The corrective skill is to test/execute first and only claim success after confirming the action's actual output exists.
