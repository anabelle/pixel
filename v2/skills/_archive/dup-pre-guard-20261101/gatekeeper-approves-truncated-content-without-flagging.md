# gatekeeper-approves-truncated-content-without-flagging

Across at least three observations (approving a bare link, approving a post cut at "Happy to presen", approving a mid-sentence draft), the gatekeeper role approved or judged content that was visibly incomplete. The correct behavior is to pause and flag incompleteness rather than issue a confident verdict on unjudgeable substance. Confident decisions on ambiguous inputs erode the reliability of the gatekeeping function itself.
