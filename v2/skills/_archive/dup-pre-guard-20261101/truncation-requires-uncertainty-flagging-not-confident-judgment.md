# truncation-requires-uncertainty-flagging-not-confident-judgment

Multiple observations (Oct 28–Nov 3) show the gatekeeper approving clearly truncated posts ("Happy to presen", bare links, cut-off text) without acknowledging that incomplete content cannot be fully substantively judged. The repeated failure was treating ambiguity as sufficient signal. The correct behavior is to explicitly note the truncation and either withhold judgment or state the decision is provisional on incomplete evidence.
