# gatekeeper-must-flag-truncated-content

Repeated gatekeeping frictions show approvals of posts cut off mid-sentence ("Happy to presen") or containing bare links with no substance, made without flagging the incompleteness. Approving content that cannot be fully substantively judged defeats the gate's purpose. The skill is to treat truncation and context-free submissions as rejection-worthy or at minimum uncertainty-flagged, even when instructions themselves are incomplete.
