# gatekeeper-must-flag-truncated-content

Multiple observations (2026-10-29, 2026-10-28) show the gatekeeper approving truncated or cut-off posts without noting the incompleteness. Approval on incomplete content is not a substantive judgment; it should either be deferred or accompanied by an explicit uncertainty flag. Repeated pattern across at least three judge events.
