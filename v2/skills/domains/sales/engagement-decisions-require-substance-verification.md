# engagement-decisions-require-substance-verification

Several frictions show engagement (or approval) applied to low-substance content: an unfinished engagement-bait hook accepted as genuine conversation, a vague one-line link approved without evidence of authorship, and a reply that misread the post's core angle (censorship resistance vs. conviction). The pattern is a bias toward engagement without first verifying substance and correctly interpreting intent. When context is thin or the angle is ambiguous, the decision should default to silence or clarification, not engagement.
