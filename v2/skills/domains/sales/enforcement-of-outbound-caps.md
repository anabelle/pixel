# enforcement-of-outbound-caps

Reliance on memory or "intuition" regarding outreach frequency leads to harassment patterns and brand damage. To prevent "outreach overkill," the system must maintain a strict outbound counter per user that triggers a hard stop once the protocol limit (e.g., 2 messages) is reached, regardless of conversion pressure.
