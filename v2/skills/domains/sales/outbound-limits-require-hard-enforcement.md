# outbound-limits-require-hard-enforcement

The Movimiento 360 incident demonstrated that sending 4-5 messages against a 2-message limit is not just a mistake, but a failure of enforcement mechanisms. Without a tracking system that imposes a hard stop after the defined limit, emotional pressure to convert overrides protocol, resulting in behavior that is perceived as harassment rather than persistence.
