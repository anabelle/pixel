# evaluate-substance-before-engagement-or-approval

The gatekeeper accepted an unfinished dramatic hook designed to bait replies as genuine conversation, and an assistant replied to a post while misreading its central angle (censorship resistance vs. conviction), asking a question that didn't fit. Additionally, one approval relied only on a vague reference instead of the post content. The pattern: approval/reply pipelines must verify they have the real content, assess whether it has genuine substance versus bait, and confirm the response engages the post's actual thesis rather than an assumed one.
