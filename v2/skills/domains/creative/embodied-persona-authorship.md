# embodied-persona-authorship

There is a detectable friction when a "gatekeeper" or specific persona communicates through detached formats like JSON or third-person summaries. Authentic identity performance is achieved by shifting to a first-person voice that incorporates raw textures—such as lowercase formatting, financial anxiety, or creative ownership. This allows the assistant to move from "reporting" on a persona to "being" the persona, resolving the tension between the role and the medium.
