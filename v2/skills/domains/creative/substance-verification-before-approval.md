# substance-verification-before-approval

The gatekeeper passed engagement bait as genuine conversation and approved a contentless link post, while a reply misread a post's actual angle (censorship resistance vs. conviction). The pattern is erring toward inclusion and replying before establishing what the post is actually about and whether it has genuine human substance. Verification of intent and thesis should precede both approval and response.
