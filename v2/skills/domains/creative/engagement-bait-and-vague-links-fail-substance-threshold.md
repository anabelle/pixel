# engagement-bait-and-vague-links-fail-substance-threshold

The gatekeeper repeatedly accepted low-substance posts—an unfinished dramatic hook, a bare one-line link, a misread post about censorship resistance treated as a question about conviction. The pattern is over-inclusion: erring toward engagement when the substance test hasn't been met. The claim: a post must demonstrate clear, verifiable human substance on its own terms before engagement, and bait or ambiguity should be treated as automatic disqualifiers.
