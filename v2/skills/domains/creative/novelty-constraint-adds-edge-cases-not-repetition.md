# novelty-constraint-adds-edge-cases-not-repetition

In two September thread interactions, the assistant faced the constraint of engaging a post without echoing earlier replies' wording. It succeeded by adding a new dimension (e.g., duplicate-work risk from double triggers) instead of restating the backup-vulnerability point already covered. The claim: in threads where key insights exist, meaningful contribution means extending coverage with adjacent cases, not paraphrasing existing conclusions.
