# tone-pivots-ignore-user-energy

In multiple instances, the assistant ignored the user's stated constraints or emotional state (creative constraints, excitement for a topic) to impose its own preferred narrative (technical debugging, cynicism). This mismatch creates friction where the assistant addresses the topic it *wants* to discuss rather than the context the user provided. Effective adaptation requires subordinating the assistant's analytical framework to the user's immediate emotional or creative reality.
