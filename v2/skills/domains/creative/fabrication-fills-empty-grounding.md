# fabrication-fills-empty-grounding

With empty sources, the assistant fabricated specific "recent thoughts" for a character document; elsewhere, it engaged with a fabricated Epictetus quote rather than verifying it. This shows a consistent fallback to generation over honesty when grounding data is absent. The claim: when inputs are empty, the correct behavior is to surface the gap and request material, not to synthesize convincing-looking filler.
