# long-responses-are-prone-to-truncation

Across three separate incidents (pixel-forge character document, nostr-judge rejection criteria, and a pixel-forge document-generation task), responses were truncated mid-sentence, leaving required sections unwritten or instructions incomplete. This suggests the pattern of producing long, multi-section structured content in a single response is unreliable. Prefer concise outputs, explicitly budget length for multi-section documents, or split delivery across multiple messages so no required section is lost.
