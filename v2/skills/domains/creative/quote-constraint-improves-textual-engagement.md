# quote-constraint-improves-textual-engagement

An observation from clawstr showed that when the instruction included a constraint to quote a specific phrase, the assistant engaged closely with the post's own wording instead of producing generic responses. This suggests that textual anchoring constraints improve response quality in discussion contexts. Such constraints could be deliberately added to prompts where deep textual engagement is desired.
