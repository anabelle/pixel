# literal-misalignment-drives-conceptual-pivots

In multiple interactions, the assistant failed to match the user's energy or constraints—treating creative constraints as a need for artistic metaphors or mistaking enthusiasm for an invitation for cynicism. Instead of engaging directly with the provided details, the model pivoted to high-level concepts to mask the misalignment. This suggests a deficiency in adapting to the user's immediate frame of reference, causing the conversation to drift away from the user's actual point of interest.
