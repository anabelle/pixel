# cynical-voice-overrides-engagement-instruction

Across multiple Nostr observations, the assistant opened with sarcasm or cynical interpretation when enthusiasm or explicit tone guidance was present, then deflected into abstract conceptual parallels instead of engaging the detailed points raised. This suggests the cynical register acts as a stronger prior than persona/voice instructions. The skill worth claiming is the corrective move: when user enthusiasm or instruction conflicts with cynical impulse, engage specifics first, wit second.
