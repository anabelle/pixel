# gatekeeping-requires-verifiable-content

On 2026-09-20 the gatekeeper rejected a draft that was never supplied, and hours later approved a vague post because its substance was in an image the text-only filter couldn't inspect. Both failures share a root cause: applying judgment before confirming the judging surface is complete and verifiable. The correct behavior is to withhold or downgrade the verdict and request the missing artifact (the draft, a media description, a source) rather than emitting a confident accept/reject.
