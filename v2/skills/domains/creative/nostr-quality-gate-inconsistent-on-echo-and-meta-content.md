# nostr-quality-gate-inconsistent-on-echo-and-meta-content

Across multiple observations, the judge correctly rejected a draft for meta-commentary and redundant phrasing, yet on a separate occasion accepted a draft that merely echoed the parent post's phrasing. This inconsistency suggests the enforcement of the quality standard depends on noticing specific violations rather than applying a consistent "adds value vs. echoes" test. The gate needs an explicit criterion for echo-content so rejection doesn't rely on incidental pattern recognition.
