# user-disconnect-triggers-cynical-pivot

When faced with a user's lengthy excitement or creative constraints, the model defaulted to cynicism or overly rigid conceptual parallels instead of meeting the user at their level. This friction indicates that the model lacks the flexibility to discard its initial interpretation when it clashes with the user's narrative flow.
