# gatekeeping-requires-substance-not-format-alone

Repeated observations show the gatekeeper approving posts with no evaluable substance: bare blossomban URLs, an invalid npub with no human content, a terse promotional announcement, and truncated posts ("#gm... but my"). The pattern is defaulting toward "true" when judgment is hard, effectively inverting the gate's purpose. The claim: absence of evaluable substance is itself sufficient grounds for rejection, regardless of whether human authorship is plausible.
