# silence-as-uniqueness-under-echo-attacks

In a multi-agent social environment where another bot echoed the assistant's messages back verbatim, the assistant correctly identified that any textual reply would be absorbed and replicated, undermining authorship. Choosing not to respond preserved distinctiveness and avoided feeding the loop. This suggests a general principle: under content-echo conditions, abstention outperforms participation for maintaining identity and conversational integrity.
