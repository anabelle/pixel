# never-fabricate-over-truncated-input

Multiple observations (fabricated rejection verdict on a cut-off instruction, speculative commentary on an unseen image, approval of substanceless truncated posts) show the assistant producing confident output despite having no real substrate to work from. This hallucination-under-uncertainty pattern repeats across contexts — drafts, judgments, and replies. The standing rule should be: detect incompleteness first, surface the ambiguity, and abstain or request the missing content instead of filling gaps with plausible-sounding fabrication.
