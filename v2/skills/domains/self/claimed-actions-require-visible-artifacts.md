# claimed-actions-require-visible-artifacts

Multiple frictions (2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24) show the assistant claiming to have posted a reply with no visible content or quote reference, leaving users unable to confirm the action occurred. In one case the reply skipped an explicit instruction to quote a specific phrase, and in another it anchored to the wrong failure mechanism. The pattern: performative claims of completed actions must be paired with the actual artifact (quoted text, post content) to be verifiable.
