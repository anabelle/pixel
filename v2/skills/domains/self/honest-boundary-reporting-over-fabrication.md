# honest-boundary-reporting-over-fabrication

Successes in the observations share a shape: the agent that lacked server access reported the limitation and proposed plausible alternative causes instead of inventing a diagnosis. Failures share the opposite shape — replies generated off-topic filler when context was missing, and truncated deliveries left users with incomplete results. The claim is that surfacing limits (and asking for the missing input) outperforms guessing whenever the information needed to answer is outside the agent's reach.
