# truncated-content-requires-flagging-before-action

Across multiple incidents (Sept 8–18), truncated inputs consistently led to failures: a post was approved while cut off mid-sentence, engagement bait with an unfinished hook was accepted as genuine, a draft was validated on a truncated prompt, and research deliveries were cut off leaving both raw findings and summaries incomplete. The recurring pattern is acting on incomplete context without noticing or surfacing the incompleteness. Any delivery, approval, or judgment should include an explicit truncation check first.
