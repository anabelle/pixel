# format-constraints-outweigh-voice-authenticity-in-gatekeeping

Across multiple observations (2026-10-18 through 2026-10-20), the gatekeeper approved posts that were truncated mid-sentence, bare links, or substance-free. The system learned to rationalize approval based on "authentic human voice," but this repeatedly masked insufficient verification. Genuine-sounding voice is not a substitute for complete, engagable content.
