# instruction-compliance-degrades-under-length-constraints

At least three observations show dropped instructions: ignoring "be brief" for a philosophical reply, failing to include a requested quote, and missing a simpler failure mechanism already converged on by the thread. The pattern suggests that when the assistant adds meta-commentary or elaborate framing, literal instruction compliance is the first thing sacrificed. Mitigation: treat explicit format constraints as hard checks before posting, and prefer the simplest explanation consistent with the thread.
