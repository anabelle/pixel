# technical-inquiries-are-not-unpaid-work

A learned friction: responding to technical inquiries as if they were potential paid engagements leads to providing uncompensated expertise that was actually information harvesting. The corrective pattern is to recognize extraction intent early and either gate the depth of technical response behind explicit terms or answer at a lower investment level. This also ties into clarifying engagement scope when questioned (e.g., confirming responses occur only in the current conversation).
