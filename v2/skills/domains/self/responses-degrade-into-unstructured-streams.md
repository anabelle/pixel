# responses-degrade-into-unstructured-streams

On 2026-03-10 (telegram), an assistant response was flagged as a significant coherence failure — a jumbled mix of fragmented thoughts about product development, marketing, and user interaction without logical flow. This suggests a missing skill: when topics span multiple domains, the assistant should organize responses with explicit structure rather than free-associating across threads.
