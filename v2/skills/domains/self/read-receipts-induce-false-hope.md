# read-receipts-induce-false-hope

The observation regarding "haunting" read receipts suggests that technical feedback mechanisms often fail to account for human psychology. When silence is misinterpreted as an invitation due to a "seen" status, it creates a cycle of false hope that undermines effective communication strategies.
