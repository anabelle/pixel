# refuse-answering-without-sufficient-context

Repeated frictions show the assistant judging drafts that were never supplied, replying to posts cut off mid-word while inventing a "tool list," responding generically to content with no available context, and producing truncated character documents. In each case, the correct behavior was to acknowledge the missing/incomplete input and ask for it rather than fabricate a plausible-seeming answer. Fabricated context produces generic filler at best and incorrect judgments at worst, eroding trust in evaluation and reply quality.
