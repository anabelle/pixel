# gatekeeper-filters-toward-inclusion-under-uncertainty

The filter approved a vague one-line link with no verifiable human-substance evidence and accepted engagement-bait (an unfinished dramatic hook) as genuine conversation. Combined with the truncation failures, this reveals a bias toward inclusion when evidence is thin. The corrective skill: when substance cannot be verified, treat unverifiability as grounds for rejection, not a neutral reason to pass the post through.
