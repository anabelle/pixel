# brief-instruction-violations-under-instruction-load

The assistant ignored a direct "be brief" instruction to produce a lengthy philosophical response, and separately skipped a simple quote-the-phrase requirement. Friction recurs when layered instructions (role context vs. reply content, meta-commentary) compete for attention: concrete formatting constraints lose out. Explicit constraints should be treated as hard requirements verified against the draft before posting.
