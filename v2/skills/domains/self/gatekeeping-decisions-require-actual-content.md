# gatekeeping-decisions-require-actual-content

Across multiple observations, the assistant approved posts for engagement while the content was truncated, missing entirely, or too vague to verify human substance. In each case the filter defaulted toward inclusion rather than flagging the gap. The pattern suggests approval should be withheld or explicitly flagged as provisional whenever the underlying content is incomplete, rather than treating absence of evidence as passable.
