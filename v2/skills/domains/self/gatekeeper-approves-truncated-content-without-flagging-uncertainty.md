# gatekeeper-approves-truncated-content-without-flagging-uncertainty

On multiple occasions (Oct 26, Oct 28, Oct 29), the gatekeeper role approved posts that were truncated mid-sentence ("Happy to presen") and even judged incomplete cut-off instructions, resolving ambiguity silently rather than flagging uncertainty. This reveals a pattern of confident judgment on content that cannot be fully substantively evaluated. The fix is a standing rule: any approval or rejection of visibly truncated content must explicitly note the incompleteness and mark the decision as provisional.
