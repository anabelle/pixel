# incomplete-content-requires-deferral-not-approval

Multiple frictions (2026-09-08 and 2026-09-09) show gatekeeper/guardrail roles approving or engaging with posts that were cut off mid-sentence, truncated prompts, or only referenced without content provided. Each time, the system made a judgment call on incomplete evidence instead of halting and requesting the full content. The pattern: absence of content is itself signal, and decisions made on partial inputs are systematically unreliable.
