# verification-criteria-must-preserve-human-context

The gatekeeper misclassified pithy human wit as engagement bait and rejected a genuine opinion delivered via image, revealing that verification-focused criteria over-filter when posts lack personal context. Rejection should require positive signals of bait (e.g., no substance, generic hooks) rather than merely absence of personal context. Ambiguity about human authorship should default to escalated review, not silent rejection.
