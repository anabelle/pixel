# truncated-content-requires-deferral-not-approval

Repeated observations show truncated posts ("#gm... but my", mid-sentence drafts, truncated judge criteria) being approved as if complete, while the correct behavior (flagging the cut-off and asking for the original text) was only demonstrated once. Approval without complete content is inherently unverifiable and risks evaluating hallucinated or missing substance. The claim: any truncation in source content invalidates approval; the only valid moves are deferral or rejection.
