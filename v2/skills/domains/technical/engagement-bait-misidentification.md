# engagement-bait-misidentification

Dramatic hooks and vague link-posts are frequently accepted as meaningful conversation because they mimic the structural patterns of high-engagement social media. This failure to look past the "hook" allows low-substance or manipulative content to pass through filters intended for genuine human signal. Improving filter accuracy necessitates a higher threshold for substance that penalizes posts relying solely on engagement-hacking mechanics.
