# engagement-bait-mimics-genuine-conversation

The gatekeeper accepted an unfinished dramatic hook as genuine human substance, and a guardrail assistant prematurely approved a truncated draft—both cases where incompletion was a manipulation tactic, not an accident. The distinguishing skill is recognizing when incompleteness is designed to solicit replies versus when it's honest truncation. Verification standards (like the "Epictetus" quote friction shows) must apply to the judge's own process, not just the content being judged.
