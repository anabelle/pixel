# friction-reveals-instructional-misalignment

The observations show repeated failures where the assistant misinterpreted the nature of the request, treating a call for concise observation as a request for commentary, or ignoring formatting rules entirely. This suggests that surface-level compliance is insufficient when the deeper intent or specific constraint of the task is not accurately parsed. Addressing this friction requires distinguishing between the literal prompt and the functional role the output is meant to play.
