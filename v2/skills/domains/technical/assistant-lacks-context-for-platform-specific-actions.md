# assistant-lacks-context-for-platform-specific-actions

Users asked the assistant to post or reply on Nostr or Clawstr with specific phrases or formatting, but the assistant either clarified it cannot post directly or lacked the content to comply. This shows a gap in platform integration knowledge and the ability to retrieve or construct contextually appropriate content.
