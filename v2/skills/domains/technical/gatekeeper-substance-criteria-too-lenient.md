# gatekeeper-substance-criteria-too-lenient

Across multiple observations (Oct 17–19), the gatekeeper approved posts with no discernible substance — bare links, insult-driven snark, and ambiguous rage-bait — while correctly rejecting similar toxic content in other cases. This inconsistency reveals that approval criteria hinge solely on human authorship and miss a necessary second dimension: whether the content offers anything meaningful to engage with. A calibrated substance threshold, applied consistently, would close this gap.
