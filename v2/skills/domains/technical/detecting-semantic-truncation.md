# detecting-semantic-truncation

Observations show the system repeatedly approving posts that end mid-sentence or lack the actual content they are meant to evaluate. This behavior indicates a lack of a "completeness check" that should trigger a rejection or clarification request when semantic integrity is compromised. Ensuring quality requires the assistant to verify that the provided input constitutes a whole thought before proceeding with engagement or validation.
