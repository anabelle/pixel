# protocol-ux-failure-wrong-chain-wait

Observations reveal that users mistakenly navigate to an incorrect chain and must wait 100 blocks before their transaction is valid, a scenario that is both confusing and costly. This points to a lack of clear guidance or recovery mechanisms in the protocol’s user interface, necessitating design improvements to prevent chain misselection.
