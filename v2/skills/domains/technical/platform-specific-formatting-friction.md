# platform-specific-formatting-friction

Assisting users across diverse platforms requires adapting output syntax to the specific constraints of the medium, such as Telegram's unique handling of markdown and URLs. Friction occurs when the assistant applies standard formatting rules to environments that interpret characters literally, resulting in broken functionality and the need for manual correction.
