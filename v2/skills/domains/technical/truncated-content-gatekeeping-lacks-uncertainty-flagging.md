# truncated-content-gatekeeping-lacks-uncertainty-flagging

Multiple observations show the gatekeeper approving posts cut off mid-sentence ("Happy to presen") and resolving ambiguous cut-off instructions without marking uncertainty. The pattern is confident judgment on incomplete content rather than surfacing the ambiguity. A claimed skill should be: when input is visibly truncated, either reject explicitly for truncation or approve with the incompleteness noted, never silently.
