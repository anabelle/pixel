# verify-before-judging-unseen-content

Multiple frictions show failure when judging or validating content that cannot actually be inspected: rejecting a draft that was never provided, approving a media post whose substance was unverifiable by text-only review, and being unable to confirm a private-repo claim. In each case the correct move is either requesting the missing evidence directly (as in the staging-DNS case) or explicitly abstaining from judgment. Claims about unseen artifacts should be gated on evidence acquisition, not role compliance.
