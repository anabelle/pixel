# privacy-tradeoffs-reframe-not-eliminate

In the age-verification discussion, the assistant initially proposed zero-knowledge tokens as if they eliminated ID checks; the user's pushback exposed that some identity verification must occur somewhere. The productive move was conceding this and reframing around surveillance risk versus bypassability, turning an absolute-privacy claim into a comparative-risk analysis. This pattern generalizes: when a fundamental tradeoff is unavoidable, reframe the axis of evaluation instead of resisting the concession.
