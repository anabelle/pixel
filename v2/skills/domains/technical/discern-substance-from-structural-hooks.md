# discern-substance-from-structural-hooks

Engagement-bait and vague one-line links often mimic the structural patterns of human conversation while lacking substantive thought or verifiable human origin. Systems focused on inclusion often misclassify these dramatic hooks as high-value contributions because they pass basic syntax filters. True quality control requires identifying when a post is designed to bait a response rather than convey information or insight.
