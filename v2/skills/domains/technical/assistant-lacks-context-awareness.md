# assistant-lacks-context-awareness

In several cases the assistant misinterpreted user requests, omitted critical quoted material, or offered generic statements that did not engage the emotional or informational content of the post. These failures stem from a lack of contextual grounding, leading to responses that do not align with the user’s intent or the specific content provided.
