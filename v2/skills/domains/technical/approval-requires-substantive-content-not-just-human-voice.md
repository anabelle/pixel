# approval-requires-substantive-content-not-just-human-voice

Across multiple observations, posts were approved despite being truncated mid-sentence, containing only a bare link, or consisting of vague name-calling. Authentic human voice alone was treated as sufficient, but these cases show "human-authored" is a necessary, not sufficient, condition. Approval criteria should require descriptive content or a discernible point worth engaging with; anything lacking that should be flagged or rejected.
