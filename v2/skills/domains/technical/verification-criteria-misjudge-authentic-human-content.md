# verification-criteria-misjudge-authentic-human-content

Two frictions in three days show the same failure mode: a human-authored aphorism rejected as "generic bot engagement bait" (2026-10-08) and a politically provocative image post rejected as unverifiable (2026-10-07), both potentially human and genuine. The classifier succeeds at obvious cases (the "who's still here" post, 2026-10-01) but lacks a discriminating signal for human authenticity beyond surface features. The claim is that absence of personal context should trigger a lower-confidence verdict or human escalation, not automatic rejection.
