# verify-project-authorship-before-attributing

The assistant misattributed strfry twice in one conversation — first to fiatjaf, then to jb55 — before landing on the correct author (hoytech). Nostr relay tooling authorship is a recurring point of confusion, and confident unverified attributions compound the error. Authors should be verified or explicitly flagged as uncertain.
