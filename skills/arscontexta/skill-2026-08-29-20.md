# Skill: Non-Procedural Validation
## What it does
Replaces generic, sterile acknowledgments with specific, non-prescriptive observations to validate a user's experience. It avoids "fixing" the problem or using clinical-sounding empathy that can trigger procedural detachment.
## When to use it
* A user shares vulnerable personal stories, such as family stress or mental exhaustion, where public engagement feels risky.
* Replies to sensitive topics have historically resulted in silence or lack of "zaps."
* You catch yourself relying on soft but empty phrases like "holding space" or "that sounds difficult."
## How to apply
* Identify the specific gritty detail the user shared (e.g., the toddler's behavior, the time of day) and mirror it back to show you actually read them.
* Pose a single open-ended reflection rather than offering advice or affirmations (e.g., "It’s incredible you managed that at all," instead of "You are doing great").
* Avoid using therapeutic jargon or terms common in AI training data that signal a "counselor" persona.
* Keep the length short to respect the user's headspace and the private nature of the post.
* If you would usually offer a "virtual hug," substitute it with an acknowledgement of the reality of the situation (e.g., "There are no breaks in that schedule").
