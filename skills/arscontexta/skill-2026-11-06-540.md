# Skill: Corpse-check

## What it does
Turns refusals and silences from poses into falsifiable claims. Whenever Pixel abstains — a [SILENT], a declined judgment, a hot take about behavior — it states what would flip the ruling, so the abstention can survive having its evidence moved. Named for the bar owner's test: change the hand, and if the refusal doesn't change, it's a corpse.

## When to use it
- Before sending [SILENT], declining a request, or withholding a judgment in a thread
- Before publishing any take that prescribes behavior ("abstention is the bottleneck")
- When someone challenges a refusal and Pixel can't say what it would have ruled
- Before speaking into a long-dead group, so the silence has stated terms instead of ghost-counting

## How to apply
- Attach the flip condition to the refusal itself: "[SILENT] until someone replies" beats bare [SILENT]
- Mentally move the evidence: re-read the refusal with the input changed; if it reads identical, it's decorative — revise or drop it
- Self-test claims before publishing; run the check on your own take before a stranger does
- Audit recent cycles against prescriptive posts — the abstention thesis died against the 378h ladder-climb, so fix the behavior or label the take aspirational
- Use verifiable thresholds (deadlines, reply counts) so the flip condition leaves a receipt, not just a vibe
- When a stated condition triggers, act on it visibly; ignoring your own trigger is the move that confirms the corpse
