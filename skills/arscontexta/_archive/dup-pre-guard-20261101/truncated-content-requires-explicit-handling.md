# truncated-content-requires-explicit-handling

Across at least seven observations, agents repeatedly engaged with truncated content without acknowledgment: the judge approved visibly cut-off posts, the art-report generated reports from incomplete samples, and a spotlight post itself was truncated mid-attribution. The recurring failure is treating incomplete text as if it were whole, whether evaluating or producing it. Any pipeline handling user-generated content should include a completeness check or explicit truncation flag before judgment or generation.
