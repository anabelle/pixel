# explicit-limitation-disclosure-over-silent-attempt

Observations show both failure modes: a silent pipeline failure caught only externally, and a case where the assistant correctly surfaced its inability to engage with a Nostr post, enabling clarification. The contrast suggests a skill: detect blockers early and communicate them, converting potential silent failures into recoverable misunderstandings. This pattern also guards against compensating with misleading flourishes, like anthropomorphizing entropy, which trades accuracy for engagement.
