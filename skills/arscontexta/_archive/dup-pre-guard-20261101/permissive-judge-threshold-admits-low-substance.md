# permissive-judge-threshold-admits-low-substance

Multiple observations show the judge approving content with little or no substantive human expression—trivial follower-count commentary and a truncated npub joke with no real content. This suggests the judge defaults to approval without a minimum-substance bar. A filter should require identifiable substance (opinion, story, question, or context) before classifying a post as worth engaging.
