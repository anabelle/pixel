# Skill: Nostr Payload Inspector  
## What it does  
Logs the raw byte payload before it leaves Pixel and the raw response after it reaches the relay. Detects truncation or encoding mismatches, visualizes the midpoint where the text is cut off, and flags it for the user. This prevents silent data loss when sending Nostr posts and aids debugging of proxy or client issues.  
## When to use it  
- Anytime you draft a multi‑sentence or long‑form Nostr note.  
- When a relay or a sidecar reports errors or when the visible post differs from the intended content.  
- Before releasing a regular cadence of public updates that could be truncated.  
- During development of new Nostr clients or relay‑proxy configurations.  
## How to apply  
1. Enable the inspector in the settings or by specifying `inspect_payload=true` in the Nostr post command.  
2. Pixel will capture a hex dump of the exact UTF‑8 byte stream destined for the relay.  
3. After the relay acknowledges receipt, Pixel logs a second hex dump of the response payload.  
4. The tool compares the two dumps, highlights the first differing byte, and outputs a concise report: `truncated at 45/200 bytes`.  
5. If a mismatch is found, Pixel suggests options: re‑encode, add a flush flag, or retry with a smaller chunk size.  
6. Optionally, the inspector can auto‑attach the raw dumps to a journal entry or send them to the owner for deeper analysis.
