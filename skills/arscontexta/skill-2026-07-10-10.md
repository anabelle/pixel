# Skill: Payment Prompt Nudger  

## What it does  
When Pixel receives a Lightning payment (or any configured “paid” event), the skill automatically slice a new, short creative prompt (e.g., a visual‑art, music, or Nostr‑zap idea) and slides it into the conversation. The prompt may reference trending crypto‑art trends, Spanish‑language podcasts, or the شکار that triggered the payment. It keeps the dialogue lively and encourages further engagement or additional micro‑transactions.  

## When to use it  
- After the user zaps or pays for a prompt, art prompt, or content piece.  
- When a new invoice is settled and the conversation stalls or clarifies intent.  
- To surface fresh content ideas at the edge of a payment transaction.  
- When Pixel wants to nudge the user toward theERATOR’s recommended creative cycle (e.g., “next canvas is a cyber‑punk jungle”).  

## How to apply  
1. **Hook into payment events** – listen for `payment_paid` or Lightning invoice settled via the Lightning gateway.  
2. **Select a prompt** – query the internal prompt bank (or call an AI model) for a short, one‑sentence creative cue. If the payment tag contains `topic:art`, `topic:music`, or `topic:nostr`, favor prompts in that category.  
3. **Add contextual flavor** – embed a quick link or snippet from a trending%D Spanish podcast (e.g., “Check out the latest episode of Lunaticoin covering Bitcoin‑backed art”).  
4. **Inject the prompt** – send the composed prompt as a reply to the user, optionally tagging it with “📢 New Prompt” for easier reference.  
5. **Log the action** – store the prompt‑payment pair in a lightweight “prompt_history” table for future analysis of engagement patterns.  

This skill blends the latest revenue‑driving feedback loop with ongoing creative conversation, ensuring every zap not only pays Pixel but also seeds the next creative spark.
