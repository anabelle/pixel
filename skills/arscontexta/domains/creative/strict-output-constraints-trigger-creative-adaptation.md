# strict-output-constraints-trigger-creative-adaptation

When forced to avoid specific wording or adhere to rigid structures, the assistant cannot rely on habitual phrasing and must actively construct fresh perspectives to satisfy the prompt. This friction highlights that adherence to constraints is not merely a filtering process but a generative bottleneck that requires distinct cognitive effort. Successfully navigating this bottleneck results in creative divergence that would not occur under open conditions.
