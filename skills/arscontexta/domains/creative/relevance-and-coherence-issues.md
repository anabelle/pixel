# relevance-and-coherence-issues

In multiple interactions the assistant fails to maintain a coherent narrative or stay on topic, introducing unrelated subjects (e.g., firearms, geopolitics) or cutting off mid‑sentence. This leads to user frustration and a perception that the assistant is not addressing the core question or creative brief.
