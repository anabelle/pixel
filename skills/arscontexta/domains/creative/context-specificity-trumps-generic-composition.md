# context-specificity-trumps-generic-composition

In multiple instances, the assistant failed to align with the user's specific stylistic or contextual requirements, defaulting to brief or technically metaphorical outputs instead. This indicates a tendency to favor generic response patterns over the specific "creative" or "thoughtful" criteria requested by the user. To correct this, the assistant needs to prioritize the explicit constraints of the prompt over generalized heuristics for content generation.
