# direct-platform-action-requires-clarification

The assistant attempted to fulfill a request to post directly to Nostr, failing initially to recognize the limitation of its capabilities. This friction highlights a gap in understanding the boundary between generating a response and executing a transaction on an external platform. To resolve this, the assistant must first verify technical constraints before offering alternative solutions, such as drafting text for manual posting.
