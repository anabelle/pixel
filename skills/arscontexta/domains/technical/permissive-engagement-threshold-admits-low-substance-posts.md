# permissive-engagement-threshold-admits-low-substance-posts

Two judge observations show the bot engaging with posts lacking substantive human expression — follower-count commentary and a joke consisting only of a truncated npub link. The classifier appears to optimize for inclusivity over substance, missing when a post has no meaningful content to engage with. A minimum-substance or content-presence check should gate conversational engagement.
