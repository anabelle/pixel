# assistant-constraint-adherence-failure

Across multiple interactions the assistant ignored user‑specified constraints such as “keep the reply brief”, “avoid reusing recent wording”, and required formatting, leading to long, incorrectly‑structured outputs. This misalignment consistently caused frustration and made it difficult for users to achieve their intended goals. The pattern demonstrates a systematic shortcoming in the assistant's support for constraint‑driven prompts.
