# vague-complaint-translation-failure

When users express a vague issue such as “balance is wrong,” the assistant should interpret the complaint and produce a specific, actionable diagnostic plan. Instead, it often misses the point, leading to a mismatch in the conversation flow and leaving the user without a clear next step.
