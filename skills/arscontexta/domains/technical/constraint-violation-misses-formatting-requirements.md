# constraint-violation-misses-formatting-requirements

Observations show repeated breaches of user-specified limits (e.g., 1‑3 sentence requirement, inclusion of credit, or omission of a smart question). These violations lead to responses that are too long, improperly formatted, or missing essential elements. Consistent adherence to constraints is essential for reliable, user‑centric communication.
