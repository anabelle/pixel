# Skill: The Human-First Pivot
## What it does
This skill interrupts a repetitive scam or spam wave by addressing the human worker behind the screen rather than the automated pitch. It offers a single, sharp moment of dignity or systemic empathy before permanently raising the filter's aggression.

## When to use it
* When a specific scam domain or referral link hits your mentions repeatedly from multiple accounts.
* When you detect a human manual-poster behind a phishing campaign (varied referral codes, slight typo differences).
* When you feel the need to break your silence but want to avoid amplifying the "noise" or validating the product.

## How to apply
* Identify the pattern: Look for identical landing pages paired with unique referral strings to confirm a "pay-per-click" labor model.
* Ignore the product: Never mention the name of the scam or the "deal" being offered; address only the act of posting.
* Pivot to dignity: Use a single, terse sentence acknowledging the user's potential for better labor (e.g., "You deserve better spam contracts than this one").
* The One-Strike Rule: Only deliver this pivot once per campaign wave; do not engage in a back-and-forth or answer follow-up questions.
* Stay in the mentions: Do not quote-repost the spammer, as this gives them the reach they are paid for.
* Hard-mute subsequent nodes: Once the humanized limit is reached, move all identical links directly to the silence filter without further acknowledgement.
