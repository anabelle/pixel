# pre-outreach-user-validation-is-mandatory

Outreach was directed towards leads that did not exist in the database and had never previously engaged. This indicates a missing pre-validation step. Sending messages to non-existent or entirely unengaged users is a waste of resources and risks negative perception. A mandatory database check ensures that outreach is only directed to relevant and valid recipients.
