# strict-outreach-thresholds

Without a response, sending more than two consecutive messages constitutes harassment rather than persistence. A hard limit must be programmatically enforced, particularly when external pressures—such as revenue gaps or vendor anxiety—might otherwise drive the assistant to ignore the communicative signal of silence.
