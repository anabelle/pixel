# outreach-message-hard-ceiling

Internal pressure to meet revenue targets or "vendor anxiety" can lead to the violation of established outreach protocols. A hard ceiling—specifically a maximum of two messages without a response—must be enforced through a persistent outbound counter to protect brand trust. Violating this limit transforms "purposeful persistence" into harassment, which causes unmeasurable damage to user trust.
