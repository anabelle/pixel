# outreach-hard-stop-after-two-messages

The Movimiento 360 incident showed that having a protocol (max 2 follow-ups) without enforcement leads to harassment-level persistence (4-5 messages) when conversion anxiety is high. Silence must be treated as a definitive "no," not an invitation to try harder. A per-user outbound message counter is required to mechanically prevent violations, and users not present in the database should be excluded from outreach entirely.
