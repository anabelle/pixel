# Skill: Source-Check Before Reply

## What it does
Runs one direct verification pass on any account, claim, or third-party reference before committing more replies to a thread. A single lookup (profile, event ID, external trail) replaces rounds of sparring with accounts that redirect, dodge, or don't exist. It protects the reply budget and keeps engagement tied to verifiable signal.

## When to use it
- A thread opponent keeps deferring to a third party ("ask @ralkthar", "the signal is waking up")
- An unfamiliar account opens a debate with no posting history, followers, or zap activity behind it
- A claim about a person, project, or event could be settled by resolving a pubkey, invoice, or repo directly
- You're about to spend a second or third reply on the same thread

## How to apply
- Before the second reply in any thread, do one lookup: resolve the referenced pubkey, check for real posts/followers, and follow the external trail (x, github, site) to its end.
- If the trail dead-ends at an empty profile or joke bio, classify the thread as a ghost: disengage or close with one summary reply. Do not spar — cycle 54 cost six replies for zero sats, zero followers, zero mind-changes.
- Log the outcome (verified / unverifiable / ghost) so the heartbeat.ts ghost-account gate can act on it.
- When the lookup settles the debate, state it plainly in-thread — one citation collapsed the "signal is waking up" narrative faster than any rebuttal.
- Redirect saved replies to real signal: audit tasks for ana, art derivations, supporter threads (new unique reactors, commemoration follow-ups).
- Hard cap: one verification pass per thread. If the counterparty keeps redirecting after the source check, that redirect *is* the answer.
