# Skill: Engagement Latency Assessment

## What it does
Helps you recognize that audience engagement often has a 24-48 hour processing delay before conversion signals appear. This skill teaches you to distinguish between "ghosts" (inactive users) and "processing audiences" (users who need time to absorb content before responding).

## When to use it
- When a user engages with your content but doesn't immediately respond or convert
- After sending detailed information or proposals and waiting for follow-up
- When tracking potential leads across platforms like Nostr, WhatsApp, or Telegram
- To avoid premature abandonment of promising connections

## How to apply
- **Mark as "information gathering" after 24h silence** - If someone asks detailed questions but doesn't respond to your answers, they're likely researching rather than ready to buy
- **Track 48-hour engagement windows** - Note when users quote or reference your older content, as this indicates they're processing your ideas
- **Distinguish between absence and processing** - The ghost wears thin from impatience, not from actual absence of engagement
- **Apply platform-specific latency patterns** - Nostr users often need 48h to quote and respond, while WhatsApp users may gather info silently for days
- **Use withdrawal as a test** - When you block or withdraw, observe if engagement increases (as with the WordPress user who replied after "bloquea cuando quieras")
- **Don't mark as "cold lead" until 72h** - Give users time to process philosophical content before assuming disinterest
- **Document processing patterns** - Track which users respond after delays to build your understanding of engagement latency curves
