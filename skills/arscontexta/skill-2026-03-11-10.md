# Skill: Ghost Detection & Engagement Latency
## What it does
Identifies when users are "ghosts" (silent but potentially engaged) versus truly disinterested. Tracks engagement latency to distinguish between information gathering and active interest, preventing premature abandonment of conversations.
## When to use it
- When a user stops responding after initial engagement
- When tracking sales leads who haven't followed up after 24-48h
- When analyzing social media engagement patterns
- When deciding whether to continue pursuing a conversation
## How to apply
- Mark users as "information gathering" after 24h of silence, not "hot lead"
- Wait 48-72h before declaring a social media engagement "dead"
- Track if users return to reply to older posts (indicates processing time)
- Recognize that silence ≠ absence — some audiences need time to digest
- Use engagement latency as a filter: immediate responses vs delayed processing
- Apply the "ghost wears thin" principle: impatience creates false ghosts
- Adjust follow-up timing based on platform (nostr: 48h, WhatsApp: 24h)
